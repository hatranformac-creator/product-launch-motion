# AIMarketing launch film: director's plan

**Product:** AIMarketing by AIPOWER, a marketing operations platform with AI built in.
**Story in one line:** marketing is split across nine tools; AIMarketing turns brief → AI draft → QC →
approval → publish → measure → AI cost into one continuous flow.
**Payoff:** one click publishes to every channel (the drop), and every result comes back to one dashboard.

**Format:** 60 s, 1920×1080, 60 fps, English. **Music:** original score, 120 BPM, A minor / C major,
Am–F–C–G. Build 28–34 s, drop on 34 s (Publish), resolve to C major on 54 s (logo).
One beat is 0.5 s, so the film has 120 beats (30 bars).

**Brand:** navy #19479D, sky #228ECD, green #24B24B (logo ring), product teal #13795B (buttons in the
real dashboard), light canvas #F4F7FC with pale blue/green light pools, the deck's blue wave in the
corner on the open and close. Montserrat (brand display), Inter (UI).

**Customer example carried through:** "Saigon Roast", a fictional coffee brand, writing
"Cold brew at home: a beginner's guide".

A step rail at the top (Brief · Draft · QC · Approve · Publish · Measure · AI cost) runs from 12 s to
54 s with a stretchy liquid indicator, so viewers always know where they are in the flow.

## Real vs invented
- Product capabilities, workflow steps, status names, provider list, roles: all from the deck.
- Numbers (1,240 published, 92.4/100, 24K/1.2K/2K/4K, 315,502 tokens, $2.32): from the deck's demo
  dashboard; shown as illustrative, per your instruction.
- Invented: Saigon Roast, the cold brew article copy, Linh the approver, the 8.4K tooltip, the
  provider split percentages, the "+3 internal links" detail.
- Platform names appear as text with letter icons. No third-party logos are used.
